WORKSHOP EXPANDED

Walk. Carry. Grow.

Tap a machine or the floor and your character walks there, avoiding obstacles. The cyan ring and arrow mark your destination. Dragging switches to direct control and stops when you let go. Keyboard movement works too.

  • Carry goods Material Yard → Processor → Packing Bench → Delivery. Stopping on a work ring automatically collects, loads or picks up. After you unload at the Processor or Packing Bench you keep only unloading, so you can stack up lots of materials; leave the ring and return to pick up the output.
  • Stand 1.3s on the ring of the Gear Bench, Hiring Desk, Quality Lab or Expansion Gate to buy. No repeat purchases in a row: leave the ring and come back.
  • You can expand once you have both enough deliveries and enough cash. 5 chapters × 5 levels each.
  • Make use of restocking, cooling, cleaning puddles, collecting and recycling scrap, delivery chains, and regular and rush orders.
  • Chapter 2: 2nd Processor. Chapter 3: Paint Booth for higher value. Chapter 4: parts conveyor. Chapter 5: Export Truck for extra bonuses.
  • Staff have carry limits too, and processing and packing speed caps your income. If machines overheat, go to Cooling.
